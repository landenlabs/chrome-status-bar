Page Status Bar – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Page Status Bar is a Chrome extension by LanDen Labs (Dennis Lang). It adds a status bar to web pages that shows how many links and images a page has, details about the image or video under the mouse pointer, and the address of the link under the mouse pointer.

In short: Page Status Bar does not collect, store, sell or share any personal or browsing data. Everything it does happens locally in your browser.

Data the extension collects

None. The extension does not send any information to the developer or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code, and it makes no network requests of its own.

Information the extension reads on your device

To show the status bar, the extension reads parts of the page you are viewing. This information is displayed to you and is never transmitted anywhere.

InformationWhy it is usedWhere it is kept
Links, images and videos on the current page (addresses, sizes, file types, alt text, playback state) To show counts and hover details in the status bar Not saved; read only while the page is open
The list of links or images, when you click “Links” or “Images” in the bar To show the full list in a new tab Chrome’s in-memory session storage (chrome.storage.session); deleted when the list tab is closed, and cleared when the browser closes
Your status bar position setting (top or bottom) To remember your choice Chrome sync storage (chrome.storage.sync), which Chrome may sync between your own signed-in browsers. The developer cannot access it.

Permissions

  • Access to all sites – needed so the status bar can appear on every page you visit and read the page’s links, images and videos, including videos embedded from other sites.
  • Storage – used only for the position setting and the temporary link/image lists described above.

Sharing and sale of data

No user data is sold, transferred or shared with third parties, used for any purpose unrelated to the extension’s single purpose, or used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension removes its saved setting. Temporary lists are removed automatically when their tab or the browser is closed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions or concerns can be raised on the project’s issue tracker: github.com/landenlabs/chrome-status-bar/issues. You can also visit landenlabs.com.
